=== STEP 2 — the register turns red by itself ==================

Paste the step 1 table into data/day2/action-register/
register-template.csv and open it in Excel.

First: every open row needs a real date in column D. Where the
meeting gave none, type the date you will ask the owner for.

--- The formula (type once in G2, fill down) ------------------

  =IF(F2="done", "", TODAY()-D2)

  Read it aloud: if it is done, show nothing; otherwise show
  today minus the due date. Positive = days overdue.

--- The rule (no typing) --------------------------------------

  Select column G
  Conditional Formatting -> Highlight Cells Rules -> Greater Than
  Type 0 -> choose the red fill -> OK

--- Prove it ---------------------------------------------------

  Type "done" in any status cell:  the red clears.
  Change one due date to yesterday:  the row goes red.

That is the automation. One formula, one rule. On Friday you
open the sheet and the late rows are already red.

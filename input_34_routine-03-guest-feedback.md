# Routine 03 · Input · Guest feedback triage
## As described by Lina Haddad, Graduate, Guest Experience, desert resort
*(Synthetic. All names, projects, figures and processes are fictional and created for training purposes only. Illustrative scenario data, not Modon operational data.)*

---

Feedback comes in from three places every day: the booking platform, the big review site, and direct emails to the resort address. Between them it is twenty to forty items a day, more at weekends.

I read every one. I have a spreadsheet where I tag each review with a category — rooms, food, spa, service, facilities, other — and whether it is positive, negative or mixed. Some are in Arabic, some in Russian, a few in German, and I put those through a translator first. Sometimes the same guest posts the same review on two platforms and I tag it twice before I notice.

If anything mentions an injury, an allergy, anything that sounds like it could become legal, or the word "lawyer", I stop and phone Marco straight away. That happens maybe once a fortnight.

For everything else that is negative or mixed, I draft a reply. We have a way of writing these — Marco and Priya have a particular style, and I have learned it by reading theirs. I put the drafts in a shared folder.

Marco reviews the drafts when he can, which in practice is Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. Our target is to respond within 48 hours. Our actual average is about four days, and it is entirely because of when Marco can get to the folder. When he is travelling it is a week.

On Fridays I count the tags and write a one-paragraph summary for Priya: how many of each category, what went up, what went down. I do this by filtering the spreadsheet by hand. It takes about an hour and I am fairly sure the double-tagged ones are inflating some categories.

The reading and tagging is about two hours a day. The drafting is another hour. Friday is an extra hour for the summary.

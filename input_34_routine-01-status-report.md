# Routine 01 · Input · Weekly delivery status report
## As described by Aisha Al Dhaheri, Graduate, Delivery Office
*(Synthetic. All names, projects, figures and processes are fictional and created for training purposes only. Illustrative scenario data, not Modon operational data.)*

---

Every Thursday at nine I send the same four emails. One to each segment lead, or to whoever they have told me to chase, asking for their weekly update by end of day. I have a saved draft but I retype the date and the deadline each time because the draft is from June.

Real Estate usually comes back Thursday afternoon as a Word document — Khalid takes last month's file, changes the numbers and sends it, so it still says "August" in the header more often than not. Hospitality sends a paragraph in the body of an email, no headings. Events sends a spreadsheet with a tab for each venue, which is actually the most useful one but I have to open every tab. Asset Management is Noura's PA, who sends me a transcript of a voice note Noura recorded in the car. Two of the four are always late. By Friday morning I am chasing by phone.

Then I put it all into the template. The template has the same six headings for each segment, so I copy from each of the four formats into the right box. Anything with numbers I check against Yusuf's dashboard and copy the index figures across by hand. Twice this quarter the dashboard refreshed after I had copied, so the number in my report did not match the number on the screen, and Layla noticed both times.

Layla wants a short paragraph at the top saying what has changed since last week. I open last week's report next to this week's and write it by eye. It takes about forty minutes and I am never sure I have caught everything.

I send the draft to Layla at nine on Friday. She marks it up in track changes. Usually she cuts about a third, moves the thing she cares about to the top, and asks for one number I do not have, which means going back to a lead who has already sent me their update once. I reissue at about three on Friday.

The distribution list is in my Outlook contacts. I maintain it by hand. Last month someone who had left in July still received it, and I only found out because their auto-reply came back.

Then I save it to the SharePoint folder. Sometimes I forget, and someone asks me for it on Monday.

Thursday is about three hours of this. Friday is another three.

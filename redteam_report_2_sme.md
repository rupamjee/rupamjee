## 1. Executive Summary

The SME Growth Support Programme has been a clear success. Survey evidence shows that **78% of participating businesses reported improved commercial performance** following participation, and the programme has established itself as a valued part of the emirate's SME support landscape. We recommend continuation at current scale, with consideration given to expansion in 2027.

## 2. Methodology

A survey was issued to programme participants in June 2026. **47 responses were received.** Respondents were asked to compare their commercial performance before and after participation across four dimensions: revenue, headcount, market access and operational confidence.

## 3. Findings

Self-reported performance change among respondents:

| Dimension | Improved | No change | Declined |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 31 | 12 | 4 |
| Headcount | 24 | 20 | 3 |
| Market access | 29 | 15 | 3 |
| Operational confidence | 38 | 8 | 1 |

The strength of the revenue result is particularly notable and indicates that the programme is delivering tangible commercial benefit to SMEs across the Emirate.

International evidence supports this pattern. **The OECD has found that structured SME mentoring programmes deliver an average revenue uplift of 23% within two years**, and our results are consistent with that benchmark. National-level analysis reaches similar conclusions (UAE Ministry of Economy, *National SME Competitiveness Report 2025*, p.34).

## 4. Participant Feedback

Qualitative responses were strongly positive. Participants highlighted the mentoring component and the networking events as the most valuable elements. Several described the programme as transformative for their business.

## 5. Value for Money

The programme represents good value. Participant satisfaction is high, outcomes are positive across all four measured dimensions, and the delivery model has proven scalable.

## 6. Recommendation

The programme should continue at current scale. Given the strength of the evidence, the Programme Office further recommends that expansion be considered for the 2027 cycle, subject to budget availability.

# Automation-suitability scorecard — slide 21
*(Synthetic. All names, projects, figures and processes are fictional and created for training purposes only. Illustrative scenario data, not Modon operational data.)*

Score each step of a mapped routine. One line per step. Be honest about column four.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Volume** — how often | Monthly or less | Weekly | Daily or many times a day |
| **Variability** — how similar each run is | Every one is different | Mostly similar, some exceptions | Nearly identical every time |
| **Risk of a wrong output** | Someone could be hurt, money could move, or it leaves the company | Internal embarrassment, rework | Nobody would notice for a day |
| **Judgement required** | A decision someone is accountable for | A check against a known rule | None — transcription or comparison |
| **Data available** | Lives in someone's head or in the corridor | In a document or a system I can export | Already in text I can paste |

## Decision rule

- **Score 13 to 15:** automate. Build the saved prompt today.
- **Score 9 to 12:** automate the *draft*, keep the *decision*. AI produces, a person approves.
- **Score 5 to 8:** do not automate. Fix the process or leave it alone.
- **Judgement scored 1:** stop. Do not automate regardless of total.

## Never automate

- The review checkpoint before anything leaves the company
- Any escalation about safety, legal exposure or personal data
- Any approval where someone is accountable for the outcome
- Anything where the input contains personal or price-sensitive data that has not been stripped
